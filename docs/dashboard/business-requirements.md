# Dashboard & Analytics — Business Requirements

## Purpose
Provide users with an instant, clear, and comprehensive financial cockpit and analytics center: current net balance, period income and expenses, budget health, spending breakdowns by category, and activity over time.

## Functional Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Scope |
|----|-------------|-------|
| BR-DSH-01 | Current balance calculation: `Total Income - Total Expenses` (cumulative all-time). | Backend & UI |
| BR-DSH-02 | Period selection: support filtering summaries by Current Month, Previous Month, Current Year, and All Time. | Full Stack |
| BR-DSH-03 | Period metrics: Total Income, Total Expenses, Net Savings (`Income - Expenses`), and Savings Rate. | Full Stack |
| BR-DSH-04 | Spending breakdown by category with monetary amount and percentage of total expenses. | Full Stack |
| BR-DSH-05 | Income breakdown by category with monetary amount and percentage of total income. | Full Stack |
| BR-DSH-06 | Overall budget health summary (total planned, total spent, remaining, overall percentage). | Full Stack |
| BR-DSH-07 | Recent transactions widget showing latest 5-10 transactions with one-click navigation to history. | Full Stack |
| BR-DSH-08 | Monthly financial activity trend: comparison of monthly income and expenses across recent months. | Full Stack |
| BR-DSH-09 | Quick action shortcuts on the dashboard to immediately open dialogs: "+ Add Income", "+ Add Expense", "+ Set Budget". | Frontend |
| BR-DSH-10 | Responsive visualizations that adapt seamlessly to mobile and desktop screens. | Frontend |
